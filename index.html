Hello there!

My name is Othilie. I am a master student in cyber security, currently writing a thesis in the field of Post Quantum Cryptography (PQC).
I started in this field after a newfound love for coding and a slight paranoia. The field of cyber security is ever changing, so there is always something new to learn.
Aside from studies, i have been active in the student community. From 2023-2026 i have been the leader of the student organization HeltSikker, making Capture-The-Flag competitions, arranging speaker events and security evenings. I have also been a bit on the stage myself as a speaker at Otocon, talking about security on social media.
I really enjoy participating in the security community, learning more and challenging my views.

Outside of being a student, I work as IT-support at the University of Bergen, stumbling through cables and looking at different software. Through my work i have gotten used to looking at and getting to know completely new programs.
When I have a breather away fom the screen, You would either find me doing some crafts, on a hike or with my nose buried deep in a good book. A book i can really recommend is "Ekko: et essay om algoritmer og begjær" by Lena Lindgren.
Feel free to reach out on my LinkedIn if there are any questions!
